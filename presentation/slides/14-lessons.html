Engineering lessons

Scripts

Agents make the technical judgment calls. Scripts enforce everything else.

Flow

Enforce phase order — no step skipped or repeated by accident.

Format

Enforce the output shape (JSONL), so a response is parseable before anything reads it.

Content

Enforce that required fields are actually present, not silently omitted.

Persistence

Enforce that state and history survive a crash or a resume.

Keep salvage separate from re-review. Record what actually happened, including degraded seats.
