What the tool does not establish

Consensus

No completeness guarantee

Three seats can still miss the same defect, or the same code path.

No majority authority

A 2–1 split is disagreement, not truth — it goes to the human.

No autonomous approval

The tool surfaces evidence. A human decides what to fix or accept.

Cost and latency

More model calls, retries, rounds — more time and tokens than one review.
