What a seat returns

Review and debate claims

Initial review · one or more findings

  • Claim: one sentence, the suspected defect.
  • Location: file + line, or a precise anchor.
  • Evidence points: concrete assertions — trigger/precondition, impact.
  • Metadata: category and severity.

The seat reports findings — it does not decide the issue’s final state.

Debate · one response per issue

  • Issue ID: links to the blind card.
  • Stance: support or reject.
  • Rationale: technical reasons for or against.
  • Optional revision: corrected claim, location, category, severity.
  • New findings: additional concerns found while reviewing.
Comes with rationale and evidence, and is never tallied into a majority.

Seats never see reviewer identities, agreement counts, or an autonomous fix decision.
