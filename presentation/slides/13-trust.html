Trust model

No “security theatre”

What the protocol protects

  • Independent first-pass reviews.
  • Hidden origins and stance tallies.
  • Consistent response shapes.
  • Recoverable state and visible outcomes.

What it cannot guarantee

  • A seat cannot be prevented from reading available files by prompts alone.
  • External tools may drift in model, path, or runtime behavior.
  • Tracked-tree restoration detects honest changes; it is not confinement.
  • The run should be isolated because seats execute with broad permissions.
Targets model confusion and operational accidents — not a deliberately malicious participant.
