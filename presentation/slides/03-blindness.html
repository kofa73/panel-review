The key design choice

Blindness

Round 0

Independent first pass

Same scope, no other seat’s findings.

Debate

Technical evidence only

Blind issue cards — no origin, no stance tally.

Referee

Private attribution

Retains origins to audit, never exposes them to seats.

An information-flow rule: track who said what, keep it out of the argument.

Reduces social anchoring and imitation — not independence, and not protection against a malicious seat.
